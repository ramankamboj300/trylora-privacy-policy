Privacy Policy

Trylora: AI Clothes Changer & Photo Studio
Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Trylora mobile app (package com.trylora.app) and its related services ("Trylora", "we", "us") collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and how you can delete it. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

In short
  • We use your photos only to create the edited images you ask for.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • You can delete your history in the app, and you can delete your whole account in the app or by email.

2. How your photos are processed

  • When you start an edit, the photo, any reference image and your prompt are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our servers and to a third-party AI image-processing provider that works for us under contract.
  • The provider uses them only to create your result. It may not use them for its own purposes.
  • We do not use your photos, faces or prompts to train AI models.
  • We do not use your photos for facial recognition or to identify you.
  • Uploaded photos and generated images are kept on our servers so you can see your history. They are deleted within 30 days after you delete them or delete your account.
  • Your history and saved faces are also stored privately on your device. They are removed when you clear history, delete your account or uninstall the app.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos / media: to let you pick a photo to edit and to save results to your gallery if you ask.
  • Camera: only if you choose to take a new photo.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a result is ready and to send reminders and offers. You can turn these off in your phone settings.
  • Internet: to process images and sync your account.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that help us run Trylora, and only as needed:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, Google Sign-In): sign-in, notifications and app settings.
  • AI image-processing provider: to create your edited images (see section 2).
  • Cloud hosting provider: to store account data and images on secure servers.
  • Payment providers (Google Play Billing, Razorpay): to process purchases. They handle your payment details under their own privacy policies.
  • WhatsApp messaging provider: only if you add your WhatsApp number and opt in.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect users' safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business. In that case this policy keeps protecting your data. We never sell your personal data.

5. Advertising

Trylora does not currently show third-party ads. If we add ads later, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile data: while your account is active.
  • Photos and generated images on our servers: until you delete them or your account, then removed within 30 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting laws require, even after you delete your account.
  • Backups: removed from backups in their normal cycle, within 90 days.

7. Security

All data is sent over HTTPS. Access to our servers is limited and protected. Login sessions are kept in your phone's secure storage. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you about a breach if the law requires it.

8. Deleting your data and account

Delete history only

Open Profile → Settings → Clear history, or delete single items from the History tab.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Trylora and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Delete your account without the app

Email support@trylora.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Trylora account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account, profile, interests, WhatsApp number, uploaded photos, generated images, history and notification tokens. We keep only purchase and transaction records that the law requires us to keep. Any credits or active subscriptions are lost when the account is deleted. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play first.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/UK under GDPR, in California under CCPA/CPRA, or in India under the DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, download or delete your data, to object to or limit some uses, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, email support@trylora.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. When they do, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, as the law requires.

11. Children

Trylora is not meant for children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app.
